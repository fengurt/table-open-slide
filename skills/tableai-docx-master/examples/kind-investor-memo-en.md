# KiND · AI for all, not for a few.

**Why KiND**

AI shouldn't belong exclusively to a handful of tech giants. KiND re-architects AI into an employable **Kind Workforce.** By flexibly integrating three core capabilities—**Brain, Body, and Mind**—based on desired outcomes, we provide growing organizations, communities, and individuals with accessible, affordable intelligence.

*AI for all, not for a few.*

---

**Why Now**

The window for the Intelligence Revolution is wide open. Following the agricultural, industrial, and service sectors, AI is making intelligence itself a scalable, affordable means of production for the first time. Three critical trends are converging:

- **Compute:** Large model API prices have plummeted by 90%+ in the last 18 months. Inference costs are approaching zero.
- **Hardware (The Carrier):** Chinese manufacturing has driven AI terminal costs down to 1/3 to 1/2 of those in the West. By 2026, domestic shipments of humanoid robots will exceed 35,000 units (3x the combined total of US OEMs). Furthermore, the 15th Five-Year Plan has designated Embodied AI as a fast-tracked, strategic industrial sector.
- **Demand:** The global PR market is expanding from $880B to $1,290B (2026). The AI Avatar market is surging from $98B to $1,426B (a 30.7% CAGR). Edelman's Global CEO has publicly declared that "the era of billable hours is ending."

The traditional 4A agency model is collapsing, and pure-play software AI companies are only fighting half the battle. We are in the narrow window for a new species to emerge.

## III. Revenue: Sources, Audience, and Unit Economics

Year 1 revenue is projected across three modules.

### Module 1: Brain — AI-Native PR & Marketing Digital Workforce (Led by Tianyi Zhang)

**What We Do:** We develop fully mature "digital marketing teams" (specialized agents collaborating across Strategy, Creative, Copywriting, Media, and Content Production). This replaces the most labor-intensive, un-moated, and non-compounding headcount costs in the marketing industry. We bill based on results, not billable hours.

**Industry Context:** The global PR market will reach ~$1,290B in 2026. AI Marketing Agents have emerged as a standalone category in Q4 2025–Q1 2026. Overseas counterparts (like AuthorityTech's delivery-based billing and Salient PR's founder-led model) have already validated the replacement of traditional 4A monthly retainers with "AI-native agencies."

**Why Us:** We deeply understand PR (Tianyi Zhang brings 10 years of international 4A experience, having managed accounts like Rolls-Royce, BMW R18, and Harley-Davidson, winning Mobius, Great Wall, and Golden Mouse awards). We understand the tech (joint US-China development team). We can sell (resources on both client and agency sides), and we have already secured the iSoftStone brand PR budget as our anchor client.

**Cost Structure:**

- **Fixed Team:** 2 Account Managers (demand triage, ¥20K/mo/person) + 2 Business Definers (Tianyi Zhang, Howard; already covered in existing payroll at $75K/yr/person).
- **Agile Pods** (Activated per project, booked as COGS): Strategy ¥25K/mo + Media ¥15K/mo + Account Management ¥20K/mo. Total: ~¥60K/mo per pod.
- **Initial Costs:** Freelance strategy consultants to refine skills (amortized per case/project); 20% sales commission.
- **Efficiency Target:** Annual output of ¥750K per headcount. Once hit, we scale the team.

4. **Agent Hub:** Internal support for the PR module and external enterprise-grade deployment. Note: This continues our existing OEM business (iFLYTEK ecosystem hardware, with 100K+ units delivered).

**Why Now:** PUDU is the global market leader in commercial service robots (23% share in 2023) with over 120,000 units shipped across multiple sectors. In 2024–2025, they completed their product lines with human-like (D7) and humanoid (D9) robots. Holding master distributor/channel status means we are directly riding the product matrix of the #1 brand. Frost & Sullivan projects China's commercial service robot export market will exceed ¥5B by 2030.

**Year 1 Strategy & Projections (Base Case):**

- We have a concrete list of prospective clients. **Target:** Sign 5–10 clients within 3 months.
- Assume Year 1 LTV per client is ¥80K–250K (Selling 5–20 units OR leasing 10–30 units).
- **Revenue Breakdown:** Robot Sales/Leasing ~$400K (Sales margin 18%, Leasing margin 45%, Blended ~30%) + Existing OEM ~$345K (Margin 30%) + Custom Hardware/Software ~$100K (Margin 50%).
- **Year 1 Revenue:** ~$845K. **Gross Profit:** ~$270K.

**Capital Structuring:** Leasing and RaaS models require upfront working capital. These will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis. We will apply to the Group for separate financial leasing or supply chain financing per project—using the equipment as collateral and repaying via rental cash flow. This will not draw from the current equity injection, assumes no fixed credit line, and remains strictly ring-fenced from company operating capital.
